About This Episode

An unexpected twist in Nobita and Doraemon's friendship

In this exciting adventure, Nobita finds himself in a surprising situation when Doraemon becomes associated with Suneo. The sudden change leaves Nobita confused and worried about his special friendship with Doraemon.

Suneo enjoys having Doraemon's futuristic gadgets available to him, while Nobita tries to understand how he can bring things back to normal.

The episode brings comedy, friendship and a little competition between Nobita and Suneo, while showing just how important Doraemon is in Nobita's life.
