💙 About the Adventure

Doraemon is a robotic cat from the 22nd century who travels back in time to help Nobita Nobi. Nobita is a kind-hearted but unlucky schoolboy who often finds himself in difficult situations. With Doraemon's amazing futuristic gadgets, every ordinary day can quickly turn into an extraordinary adventure.

This episode introduces the wonderful friendship between Nobita and Doraemon and begins their unforgettable journey together.
